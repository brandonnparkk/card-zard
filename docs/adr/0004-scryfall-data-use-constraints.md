# Card data stays free and anonymous because of Scryfall's terms

Scryfall's data-use guidelines (read 2026-10-27 at https://scryfall.com/docs/api) forbid paywalling Scryfall data, require that card data be reachable anonymously or with a free account, forbid simply republishing the data without adding value, forbid implying Scryfall endorsement, and forbid cropping or altering card images (including the artist and copyright line). Card Zard is therefore free to use. LLM features get a free daily limit per user rather than a paid tier. Any future paid feature must charge for Card Zard's own added value, never for card data, and should be confirmed with Scryfall first.

## Consequences

- The Wizards of the Coast Fan Content Policy applies too, and its notice must appear in the UI.
- Rate limiting is per IP address until accounts exist, then per user.
